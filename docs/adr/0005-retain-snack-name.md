# Retain the SNACK name after preliminary trademark screening

SNACK will retain its product name and the `@snack-ai` npm scope after the preliminary INPI and USPTO screening recorded on 2026-07-30. The search found live exact `SNACK` software marks in the United States and relevant live near marks in Brazil, so this is an explicit owner go/no-go decision and risk acceptance, not a legal-clearance conclusion. Publication may proceed under the selected identity; the project will preserve the search evidence, avoid claiming exclusive trademark rights, and revisit the decision if qualified counsel, a registry dispute, or marketplace evidence identifies an unacceptable conflict.
